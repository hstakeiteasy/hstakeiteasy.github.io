Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

1. Information We Collect

At HsTakeItEasy, we collect information solely to provide a seamless educational experience. This includes student names, parent contact numbers, academic performance records (test marks), attendance logs, and fee payment histories. This data is strictly collected during the offline admission process and managed by administrators.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information collected is used exclusively for internal academic and administrative purposes, including:

  • Generating student login credentials.
  • Tracking and displaying attendance and examination records.
  • Managing monthly tuition fee billing and collection reports.
  • Sending necessary academic updates or fee reminders to parents via secure channels.

3. Data Security and Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. Your data is stored securely in our private database architecture. Access is strictly limited to authorized administrators and the individual student logging into their personal dashboard.
