Privacy Policy

For HSTakeItEasy

Last updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how HSTakeItEasy ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares your personal information when you use our mobile application (the "App") and our website (the "Service").

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1 Information We Collect

  • Personal Data: When you log in or use the App, we may collect information such as your name, email address, phone number, and student details (as provided by you or your tuition administrator).
  • Camera and Files: The App allows you to upload files (such as photos or documents) for assignments or profile pictures. We only access your camera or device storage with your explicit permission.
  • Device Information: We do not directly collect detailed device information, but standard web technologies may process basic device data strictly for security and performance optimization.

2 How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To provide and maintain the Service (including the secure student login portal).
  • To process your file uploads and allow you to download PDF reports.
  • To display important contextual information, such as your exam countdowns and daily motivation.
  • To manage your account and maintain active, secure sessions.

3 Local Storage and Cookies

We use Cookies and Local Storage to keep you logged into the App and to remember your interface preferences. This allows you to use the App seamlessly without having to authenticate every time you open it. You can clear this data at any time by clearing your App data or browser cache in your device's settings.

4 Home Screen Widget Data

Our App includes a Home Screen Widget that displays an Exam Countdown and Daily Motivation. The data required for this widget (exam name, dates, and quotes) is stored entirely locally on your device and is never transmitted to our remote servers.

5 File Downloads and Uploads

  • Uploads: When you choose to upload a photo or document through the App, we access your device's camera or storage. This access is utilized solely for the purpose of the requested upload.
  • Downloads: When you download a PDF report or receipt, the file is saved locally in your device's default "Downloads" folder. We do not have access to your personal files outside of these specific, user-initiated actions.

6 Data Security

The security of your data is our priority. The App uses secure protocols (HTTPS) to encrypt communication with our servers. However, please remember that no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7 Third-Party Links

The App may contain links to external websites and services (such as Google Drive, WhatsApp, or YouTube). If you click on a third-party link, you will be directed to that external site. We strongly advise you to review the Privacy Policy of every site you visit. We have no control over and assume no responsibility for the content, privacy policies, or practices of any third-party sites or services.

8 Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page, and these changes are effective immediately upon posting.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or suggestions regarding our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at hello@sumitaidev.com.
